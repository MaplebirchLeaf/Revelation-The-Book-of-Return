### Prerequisites and recommended order

Enable **Robin's temple route**. The PC must have passed the original entrance assessment, hold the rank of **initiate, monk/nun or priest**, remain covered and wear a chastity fitting. With deep love, no disappearance or injury, and without overwhelming trauma, Robin offers **Talk to Robin about the temple** in **Orphanage → Robin's room**.

Recommended order: **discuss the temple → meet Jordan and book the assessment → prepare and attend → work and pray together → undergo evaluation → make the promise**. The trial of anguish, food gifts, walks and confessions can fit around these steps. You do not need to finish every side scene before applying for a promise.

### Chastity assessment: booking, preparation and retrying

1. Discuss the temple in Robin's room. Give Robin time or suggest asking Jordan together. Continue the assessment conversation in the room later if needed.
2. Accompany Robin to Jordan and book the assessment **two days ahead**. The date is recorded only after confirming the appointment.
3. Before that date, choose **Go with Robin to prepare for the assessment (0:45)** in Robin's room. It is available once daily, **17:00–19:15**, with Robin at the orphanage and no tutoring or shop duty taking priority.
4. On or after the appointment day, visit **Temple → Quarters** to attend the assessment. On school days, arrive between **06:00 and 08:00**. On days without school, the window extends to **18:00**. Remain covered, below maximum stress, and ensure Robin is neither missing nor admitted to the asylum.
5. Read the result, hear Jordan's announcement and finish the response. Success opens Robin's seat and bunk, and Robin takes a temple vow.

With first virginity intact, Robin passes the ordinary examination. Otherwise, a fire trial follows. **Greater confidence and better preparation improve the chance of success.** Revisiting the result does not reroll the same appointment.

After booking, you can give Robin a **holy or stone pendant** you are wearing. This consumes the worn pendant, helps preparation and improves later contribution growth. It is a one-time gift.

After failure, offer comfort. **Seven days later**, ask Robin about trying again in the room and book another assessment two days ahead. If you previously abandoned Robin in the hall, agreement also requires deeper love, strong confidence and very low trauma. Waiting seven days alone is insufficient.

### Rite of promise: preparation, evaluation and outcome

Robin must be a temple member and your romantic partner. The PC must be a full temple member, without an active shared purification, and **not already promised to another person**. Robin must not be in a strongly doubtful outlook.

1. With Robin present, discuss the rite at the seat. Evaluation takes place by daylight, so wait if it is dusk or night.
2. Build **both contributions**, Robin's faith and shared work. Evaluation also considers PC purity and transformations, attire, your shared past and answers. Love alone does not guarantee approval.
3. Jordan checks records and questions you separately. Eavesdropping uses Skulduggery and harms the evaluation if detected. Answer according to your shared history and facts given in the conversation.
4. A strong evaluation gains direct approval. Remaining doubts prompt an extra chastity examination. An insufficient evaluation fails, requiring **thirty days** before another attempt.
5. After approval, continue at the altar, or return to Robin's seat in the evening to hold the rite. Confirm **Forever, as one** to complete the promise.

The promise makes intimacy with Robin compatible with your shared vows and shows the corresponding trait. Suitable temple fittings are removed during the rite. **Monthly examinations remain, and intimacy with other people can still break the vow.** An existing Sydney promise prevents a second temple rite. Use the Confounded Vow route below instead.

### Chastity fittings: removal and refitting

Discuss the belt at **Temple → Quarters → Robin's seat**, **11:00–20:00**, outside Sunday mass. Before promising Robin, this needs a romance, sufficient confidence and at least **two shared shifts**. An existing promise to Robin allows the conversation directly.

- Only Robin's **temple chastity belt and anal shield fitted by this route** can be handled. Another owner's equipment is not replaced.
- Ask Robin about removal. **Making the request takes fifteen minutes.** **Without a promise and with first virginity intact**, the original temple rule requires an **£80 cash donation**. The fee does not apply once promised or outside that situation.
- **During daylight, with Jordan present outside Sunday mass, the appointment can follow on the same day.** Choose **Accompany Robin to Jordan for removal (0:15)**. Jordan confirms the request and removes the fittings in the side room. **The actual appointment takes another fifteen minutes.** At dusk, at night or when Jordan is absent, the request remains pending until you return at a suitable time. - Without other fittings, Robin can also request refitting. The request takes fifteen minutes. When Jordan is free, choose **Accompany Robin to Jordan for refitting (0:05)**. Fitting and checking take five minutes. Refitting blocks the secret journey again.

Removal does not cancel Robin's temple vow or grant a second promise. Handle PC equipment through the original Jordan or keyholder options, and Sydney's through the corresponding promise or corrupt route.

### Monthly examination and shared purification

A new examination falls due about **thirty days** after promising. The journal shows the remaining days. Go to the hall and answer Jordan's summons with Robin. If Robin cannot attend, the examination waits rather than repeatedly checking the PC alone.

With intact vows and a successful examination, the original grace-based allowance is paid. Choir earnings are collected alongside it when an allowance is granted. Refusing, admitting a broken vow or failing starts shared purification. Support your companion, ask for relief or adjust your responses. Excessive pain or other conditions can require rest and another attempt. Stress collapse may lead to hospital. Resume unfinished purification at the temple.

After Confounded Vow, all three vows are examined and all three take part in shared purification. A second promise does not automatically repair broken vows. Restoration occurs through the completed purification or hospital outcome.

### Confounded Vow: a pagan rite

This route uses clues about pagan rites to attempt a second promise. The eventual blasphemous rite takes place secretly beneath the lake.

#### Prerequisites

- Hear the bishop's full account of Kylar's parents **in this save**, the revelation that awards the vanilla **Pagan Rite** feat. Discovering the manor's secret or reporting it to Jordan is not sufficient. A feat earned in another save does not replace this experience.
- Experience the Underground Compound ending in which the gaoler and witch live together, sharing her suffering, and see the vision in the prison beneath the lake.
- Reach vanilla **Promiscuity 6**, maintain romances with both Robin and Sydney, and hold exactly one promise. With **Sydney first**, complete the original promise rite, then reach a corrupt outlook. Sydney's corrupt rite is not required in this branch. With **Robin first**, complete Robin's promise rite, while Sydney must have a corrupt outlook and have completed the corrupt rite with you.
- In the Robin-first branch, finish the original corrupt rite's story. Unlocking its option, starting it or removing a chastity device is not enough. Both branches require Sydney's current **corrupt** outlook. Returning to a pure or neutral outlook prevents agreement.
- Robin has joined the temple, with deep love, sufficient confidence, low trauma and normal availability. Sydney has deep love. Preserve the existing promise. The rite does not change Sydney's outlook for you.
- All three temple vows remain intact. The PC has passed the entrance trial, without an active punishment or possession.
- **None of the three wears a chastity belt, cage or anal shield.** Request removal of Robin's temple fitting as described above, then accompany Robin to Jordan during daylight while Jordan is available. This can be completed on the same day. Handle the PC's and Sydney's equipment too. Discussion or a pending request does not replace checking the actual fittings.

#### First journey: investigation

1. **Orphanage → Robin's room**: discuss the pagan rite, start the investigation and complete the private conversation. Robin agrees only to examine the place.
2. **School library counter, during lunch**: tell Sydney the same things. Sydney agrees only to investigate together.
3. **Lake shore, by daylight**: with the lake unfrozen and no pursuit, choose **Survey a route through the lake ruin**. The survey is recorded and need not be repeated.
4. **Lake shore → Review the route and your findings**: finish preparations after both conversations. Allow three days after the latest conversation or survey.
5. **Blood moon, 21:00–23:00**: meet both companions with an unfrozen lake, no rain, crowd or pursuit, and enough strength for the dive. Choose **Examine the stone together**, witness the response, then decide whether to return.

<span class="teal">Witnessing the response grants no new promise by itself. You can try to persuade both companions to continue tonight, or return as planned.</span>

#### Continue tonight or arrange a second journey

**Robin and Sydney must both have strong lust to take part in the rite.** The initial investigation does not require it. Each companion's desire is checked separately, and one willing companion is not enough. Love, Robin's confidence and trauma, and Sydney's corrupt outlook must still meet the prerequisites.

After witnessing the response, two forms of deception use the original **Skulduggery check** and difficulty display.

- **Make the response sound like your existing promise**: downplay the unfamiliar voice as another part of the old vow. On success, both accept that account while insisting on their own names. On failure, Sydney points out the different voice and Robin refuses to disregard what was heard.
- **Claim leaving now will let it follow you**: use the lake memories to suggest that leaving an unfinished response is dangerous. This check is harder. Success makes them continue because they misunderstand their escape options, without becoming relaxed or delighted. On failure, Sydney questions the evidence and Robin recognises the PC's uncertainty.

After success, choose **Continue with both companions** only if both have enough desire. Believing an explanation does not mean wanting to participate. A companion with insufficient desire refuses explicitly, and fear cannot bypass that condition. The actual escape route, your condition and the wraith's danger still follow the original state. Failure allows only leaving together, retaining the shared observation. The check runs once when choosing the approach. Revisiting, changing language or loading the current result does not roll again.

Answering the private conversations honestly increases love, and reassuring Robin can ease trauma. Successful promise framing increases love and, when a companion responds intimately, lust. The danger framing increases Robin's trauma and lowers both companions' lust, potentially taking a barely sufficient desire below the requirement. Being caught damages love and lust, with a greater cost when a frightening lie is exposed. Retracting the claim that escape is impossible eases Robin slightly after reaching safety, but damages both companions' love. Changes use the original NPC stats and feedback beside the corresponding option or dialogue, and settle only once.

The existing romances, Sydney's current corrupt outlook and both promise-order requirements still apply. Love, confidence, trauma, desire and equipment conditions still apply. **Unyielding Will** resists ear-slime control without improving Skulduggery odds or answering for an NPC.

If you return first, allow at least three days after the shared investigation. Return to **Robin's room** and **the library counter during lunch** to hear their separate decisions. The existing promise partner considers whether that promise is still cherished, while the other considers a place beside it. Sydney also addresses jealousy and the risks of blasphemy. Changes to your relationships, outlook or vows can prevent agreement.

Once both explicitly agree, prepare again at the lake and meet on a later blood moon that meets the conditions. Both branches use the same formal rite: let each companion answer, speak each name separately, then complete the vow and return to shore. Answering for them or attempting to make the three of you inseparable aborts the attempt.

#### Outcome and interruption

On shore, confirm **Confounded Vow**, adding one promise partner. Reaching the bank eases Robin's trauma slightly. Intimate responses after a planned journey or successful promise framing increase both companions' love and lust. Continuing because of a false belief about escape grants no such intimacy reward. Investigation progress, decisions and the rite's costs do not repeat when changing language or revisiting. Further interaction is optional after a planned journey. When persuaded to continue that same night, both retain doubts and leave together before returning to everyday interaction.

With **Unyielding Will**, ear slimes cannot move your body for you, but the rite can still agitate them. Pain, stress, trauma and the wraith's response still apply, and both companions must agree separately. Without Deadwood Reblooms, or with Vanilla Plus disabled, the ordinary reaction is used.

Aborting retains the survey and any completed shared investigation, but clears both decisions and preparations. New private conversations are required before another journey. Refitting chastity equipment blocks departure and prevents continuing the rite if fitted during the journey. Pursuits, theft hostility and retaliation for lost offspring can block departure. Danger arising along the way can also interrupt the attempt. Joint temple examinations still apply.
