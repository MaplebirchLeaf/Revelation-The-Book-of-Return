### Joining and the purity trial

Enable **Robin's temple route**. The PC must be an **initiate, monk/nun or priest**, covered and below maximum stress. Robin needs **100 love**, no disappearance or injury, and trauma below 80. In **Orphanage → Robin's room → ordinary conversation**, choose **Ask Robin about the temple**. With Finance and shared housing, use **Talk to Robin** at your property while Robin is actually home and free.

Recommended route: **invite Robin → book and prepare for the purity trial → join → work and pray together → Trial of Anguish or evaluation and Rite of Promise**. The vigil and promise can progress separately. Gifts, walks and confession fit around these steps; priest preparation follows later through the duties desk.

1. Offer to ask Jordan together. Meeting and booking take **twenty minutes**; Jordan must be present in morning or daylight, outside Sunday mass. School, stalls, tutoring, shops and special plans take priority.
2. Confirm a trial **two days ahead**. Before that date, prepare together for **forty-five minutes**, once daily, **17:00–19:15**, with Robin present and enough time before the next commitment.
3. On or after the appointment date, attend at **Temple → Quarters**, or accompany Robin from home. Assessment is available **06:00–08:00 on school days**, or **06:00–18:00 without school**, while Jordan is present outside mass. Leave twenty minutes for travelling from home.
4. Hear Jordan's result and finish the response. Joining opens Robin's quarters interaction and changing area; the bunk requires a successful Trial of Anguish.

Intact first virginity passes the ordinary examination. Otherwise, a fire trial follows, with confidence and preparation improving the chance. After booking, you may give Robin a worn **holy or stone pendant** once; this consumes it, helps preparation and improves later contribution growth.

An unavailable Robin or Jordan **delays attendance without failing the appointment**. After actual failure, comfort Robin and wait **seven days** before discussing another attempt at home, then book two days ahead again. If you abandoned Robin after failure, agreement also requires **love 95, confidence above 70 and trauma below 10**.

After joining, Robin may accompany the **Sunday-night Trial of Anguish**. Enter through the temple's normal vigil conditions. With a close relationship, take Robin's hand before the fire; Sydney may join when eligible too. Each companion must succeed; PC success alone does not pass Robin.

Shared success gives Robin monk/nun rank and **Robin's bunk**, with the wardrobe and mirror. Your own bunk follows your rank. After failure, talk in the quarters, offer comfort and retry at the next permitted vigil.

With stress at **80% of maximum or above, but below collapse**, a daily half-hour rest at the bench can lead to a hospital visit. Treatment and departure depend on trauma. The assisted severe-trauma branch needs introductions to Sydney and Sirris, and either a Sydney romance or **Sydney love 40**. Continue an unfinished visit from the hospital entrance.

### Confession, promise and monthly examination

**Confession:** after you have heard **five confessions**, a temple-member Robin actually at the temple can enter the usual confessional event pool. This is a random visitor, at most once daily, rather than a separate guaranteed conversation. The topic reflects Robin's relationship, vows, experiences and Faith.

Use **Forgive / Repent / Contrition**. Their effects depend on the topic; for example, treating promised intimacy as wrongdoing may hurt the response. **Purge** and revealing your identity have their own reactions. Once your identity is known, Robin uses **three knocks** as a signal. Inviting Robin to your side requires a romance; Robin can refuse, and **trauma 40 or above** leads to leaving together instead.

**Rite of Promise:** Robin must be a member and romantic partner. Both must have joined the temple, with no active shared purification, no existing promise to someone else, and Robin's Faith no lower than −20.

1. Discuss the rite in the quarters **by daylight** while Jordan is available.
2. Build both contributions and shared work. Faith, PC purity, transformations, attire, shared history and answers affect evaluation. Eavesdropping uses Skulduggery and harms the result if detected.
3. Strong evaluation gives approval; remaining doubts may require another chastity examination. Failure requires **thirty days** before reevaluation.
4. Continue to the altar, or return to the quarters in the evening. Confirm **Forever, as one** to complete the rite.

The promise permits intimacy between you and Robin under the shared vows and removes suitable temple fittings. It does not excuse intimacy with another person. An existing Sydney promise requires the **Confounded Vow** route instead.

A monthly examination falls due about **thirty days** after the promise. Answer Jordan's summons with Robin; if Robin cannot attend, it waits. Passing with grace pays the allowance, including pending choir earnings. Refusal, an admitted broken vow or failed examination starts shared purification. Rest, hospital treatment or an interrupted purification can require resuming later. With Confounded Vow, **all three** take part; adding a second promise does not repair an existing broken vow.

### Confounded Vow

This secret route attempts a second promise with Robin and Sydney. Its Journal reminder disappears after completion.

**Required discoveries:** hear the bishop's full revelation about Kylar's parents **in this save**, reach the Underground Compound ending with the gaoler and witch together, and see the vision beneath the lake. A feat from another save does not replace these experiences.

**Required relationships and vows:** Promiscuity 6, both romances, Robin love **80**, trauma **40–79**, Sydney love **125** and a current corrupt outlook, with all three temple vows intact and exactly one existing promise. Robin must remain able to take part in normal activities. With **Sydney promised first**, the ordinary promise is sufficient. With **Robin first**, finish Sydney's corrupt rite too. None of the three may wear a chastity belt, cage or anal shield.

1. Talk privately with Robin at home, then Sydney at an available conversation spot in the library, temple or shop. Each agrees to investigate, not yet to perform the rite.
2. Survey the lake ruin **by daylight**, with an unfrozen lake and no pursuit. Finish preparation after both conversations. Wait **three days after the latest conversation or survey**.
3. Meet on a **blood moon, 21:00–23:00**, with an unfrozen lake and no rain, crowd or pursuit. The PC needs **pain below 50, tiredness below 1,500 and stress more than 40 below maximum**. Witness the stone's response together.
4. Return as planned, wait **three more days**, then hear both companions' new decisions and prepare for another suitable blood moon. Alternatively, the same-night persuasion choices use Skulduggery; their effects and difficulty differ.
5. Both companions need **lust 60** for the actual rite and must answer separately. Proceed through their answers, name each person and confirm **Confounded Vow** after returning to shore. Speaking for them or attempting to make escape impossible aborts it.

Successful persuasion does not bypass either companion's desire. A lie about danger can raise Robin's trauma, lower desire and damage trust if exposed. Current love, trauma, outlook and equipment are checked throughout; recovery below the required trauma pauses progress without deleting discoveries. Recovery after completion does not revoke the vow.

Withdrawal keeps the survey and completed investigation but clears decisions and preparation; arrange new private conversations before retrying. Wraith hostility, pursuit, refitted equipment, excessive pain, stress or tiredness can interrupt a journey. **Unyielding Will** resists ear-slime bodily control but does not remove stimulation, costs or the need for both decisions.

### After the night at the lake

After the shared rite, meet in the temple hall on a later day after 13:00, with time before 17:00 and both companions free. Share bread and tea, then compare your accounts: thirty minutes each. Their existing schedules, purification and examination requirements still apply.

Keeping these plans eases stress and tiredness and trains History. A later voice can be checked with Sydney's copy and Robin's route to the hall. English, History, Willpower or Physique offer ways to help; neither companion agrees to follow the voice alone. Completing the follow-up or leaving the questions behind removes its journal reminder.

### An echo in sleep

After completing **Confounded Vow** and the **Lost Lamb main ending**, this dream joins the pool used by ordinary nightmare-enabled sleep. It is selected alongside other dreams, with the usual sleep duration and nightmare interval, at most once a day.

The mirror brings the two memories into one room. Continue looking or wake up. Waking restores your real clothing, stats and relationships before the usual nightmare sleep settlement. The dream makes no decisions for the real companions; whether it was only imagination remains uncertain.
