### Getting started

Join the temple, remain covered and wear a chastity device. With Robin at 100 love, below 80 trauma, neither missing nor injured, choose **Talk to Robin about the temple** in **Orphanage → Robin's room**.

Arrange a purity trial two days ahead. You can help Robin prepare once a day before the trial. After failure, offer comfort, arrange another attempt or let Robin withdraw. After success, find Robin's corner through **Temple → Quarters**.

### Shifts and promises

- Robin attends the temple from 09:00 to 16:00 on weekdays without school, and from 11:00 to 13:00 on Sundays. Arrange evening visits in Robin's room first.
- Cleaning, supplies, shared prayer and mass advance shared shifts and temple contributions. Your journal records progress and the next conditions.
- Faith and doubt start at zero and have separate displays. Changes to faith first cancel the opposite value. Reaching 20 can trigger a change of outlook.
- Jordan must approve the promise rite. An existing promise with Sydney also requires unlocking a second promise. A joint promise does not bypass examinations.

### Deadwood Reblooms integrations

- **Robin expansion**: Tutoring, the shop and admission to the asylum affect availability. Temple contributions enter Robin's weekly accounts, without a separate duplicate payment.
- **Vanilla Plus**: If Robin lives with you, homeward options return to that property. Normal nights end at home, with vigil arrangements handled separately.
- **NPC sidebar portraits**: Robin wears novice clothing, changes to a monk's or nun's habit after completing the vigil, and wears pyjamas during nighttime bunk interactions.
- **More transformations**: At full transformation, visible horse tails and fish fins allow new interactions. Several forms can offer separate choices, with a shared limit of three interactions a day.
- **Temple choir**: A harpy singing for Robin can also practise singing.
