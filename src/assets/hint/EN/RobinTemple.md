# Robin · Temple life

Invite Robin to join the temple, then share trials, work and prayer. You can also discuss a promise and prepare for priesthood together.

### Invitation and the purity trial

You must be an initiate, monk/nun or priest, suitably dressed and below maximum stress. Robin needs **at least 100 love, trauma below 80** and good health. When Robin is free in the room, choose **Ask Robin about the temple**. After moving in together, you can also talk at home.

1. Offer to ask Jordan together. Visit while Jordan is free during daylight, then book a trial **two days ahead**. Travelling and booking take **twenty minutes** altogether.
2. After booking, prepare from home **17:00–19:15**, once daily for **forty-five minutes**. You may also give Robin a **holy or stone pendant** you are wearing to help preparation.
3. On or after the booked date, attend in the temple quarters or accompany Robin from home. Assessment is available **06:00–08:00 on school days, 06:00–18:00 without school**, while Jordan is present. You may return another day if you miss the time.
4. Intact first virginity passes directly. Otherwise, a fire trial follows. Confidence and preparation improve the chance of passing. After failure, comfort Robin. Discuss another attempt after **seven days**, then book two days ahead again.

Joining opens temple conversations and the changing area. Passing the Trial of Anguish makes Robin a monk/nun and opens a personal bunk.

After you have heard **five confessions**, a Robin at the temple may visit the confessional, at most once daily. Topics reflect your relationship, vows, experiences and Faith.

### Promise and monthly examinations

You can discuss a promise in the quarters during daylight when both are temple members and romantic partners, Robin's Faith is at least **−20**, and there is no unfinished shared purification or promise to someone else.

Jordan considers your contributions, Faith, purity and shared experiences. Pass the evaluation, then complete the rite at the altar. After failure, wait **thirty days** for another evaluation.

The promise permits intimacy between you under the vows. A shared examination follows about every **thirty days**. Passing and receiving an allowance also pays pending choir earnings. Refusing, admitting a broken vow or failing the examination starts shared purification.

Discuss Robin's temple chastity fittings in the quarters **11:00–20:00**, outside mass. Before a promise, this requires romance, **60 confidence** and **two shared shifts**. Talk for **fifteen minutes**, then accompany Robin to Jordan during daylight. Without a promise and with first virginity intact, removal costs **£80 cash**. You can also discuss refitting.
