# Temple duties and priest appointments

Join the rota, restore the records room and accompany a monk to investigate unusual events. With experience, you can lead assignments. Robin and Sydney can also train, help on site and prepare for appointment.

### Getting started

As an initiate, clean the upstairs quarters to **100 cleanliness**. An attendant will mention Jordan's need for help. Priests can apply directly.

In suitable clothing, **Check the temple duties book** in the hall during **daylight, 09:00–18:00**, outside Sunday mass **11:00–13:00**. After joining, complete a handover before taking outside assignments.

### Outside assignments

Four assignments open in order. The last three need monk/nun or priest rank. Complete each and report truthfully to Jordan to open the next. You can retry after failure or withdrawal.

| Assignment                  | Where to go and what to do                                                                          | First payment |
| --------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------- |
| Knocking after lights-out   | Enter the storeroom from the duties desk and check the drain and shutter                            | £60           |
| Roots beneath the records   | Meet the monk at the old dockside storehouse, mark a retreat, repel the roots and recover the books | £150          |
| The returned donation crate | Rescue the worker at the docks warehouse, repel the tentacles and bring back the records            | £200          |
| Chanting beyond the shore   | Follow the chanting from the shore, rescue the captive and investigate the woodland hideout         | £200          |

Each first truthful completion also grants **3 grace and 10 good fame**. Resolve the warehouse or shore assignment and report truthfully for **Exorcism**: active attacks and banishment deal **20% more damage to nonhuman creatures**.

With tentacles enabled, the storehouse and warehouse involve tentacle combat. A monk helps with the first investigation, then you take the lead. Surveying and preparing equipment ease the burden. With tentacles disabled, complete the rescue and compare the marks, then spend **five minutes** securing the entrance and carrying out the records.

### The shore investigation

Once you have the assignment, visit the shore in **daylight, 09:00–16:00**, and spend **ten minutes** entering the woods.

- Observe and mark a retreat. You can report to Jordan first and ask an experienced monk to meet you at the shore.
- **400 Skulduggery** lets you slip in, loosen the knots and look for a chance to lead the captive away. Approaching directly starts a fight. Observing the group or bringing help reduces the opposition. Ordering them aside needs both Physique and Willpower.
- After the rescue, report back and investigate the woodland hideout. Rescue its captives, recover records and clear the danger, then close the case with Jordan.
- Leaving keeps completed investigations and people already rescued. Invite companions again before returning.

| Preparation                               | Help                                                        |
| ----------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| Athletics or fieldwork records            | Mark a retreat and withdraw faster                          |
| History, Gwylan's advice or ward patterns | Find markings to break, reducing tentacles                  |
| Science and borrowed equipment            | Smother the burner with damp earth, weakening the tentacles |
| Physique or borrowed equipment            | Reduce fatigue when escorting the captive                   |
| Willpower                                 | Ease shock, sanity loss and fear                            |

After becoming a priest and truthfully completing the first three assignments, revisit those sites every **seven days**. Payments are **£35 / £60 / £80**, plus 1 grace, using the day's paid shift. Changed sites may still contain tentacles.

### Priest appointment

Join the rota, become a monk/nun and truthfully complete the storehouse, warehouse and shore investigations in order. You also need to:

- Rescue the missing temple member from the Danube Street manor.
- Return the golden spear to Jordan.
- Keep the temple vow and pass the current monthly examination.
- Reach 100 grace before appointment.

Ask Jordan about appointment **before 17:00**. After the interview, allow about **six hours** for the ordination trial. You can pause and continue another day. Complete all five trials. You can work with Robin and Sydney when they are taking the trial with you:

| Trial                     | Ability needed                                              |
| ------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| Lead the chanting         | 400 English or History, or choir membership and 300 Singing |
| Resist the whispers       | 500 Willpower                                               |
| Bear the censer           | 5,000 Physique                                              |
| Resume the prayer         | 400 History or 500 Willpower                                |
| Explain the investigation | 400 History or English                                      |

You can face the whispers and bear the censers together, with an attendant lending a hand. Leading and resuming the prayers, and explaining the investigation, need the relevant abilities. If you do not finish every trial, practise before trying again. Your investigations and shifts remain recorded.

After passing, take the oath for affection, fame and mental recovery. Appointment opens priest duties and prayer-room access. Monthly examinations continue afterwards.

### Robin and Sydney

Discuss appointment at each companion's temple pew. They must be monks/nuns with intact vows, their examinations and purification settled. Robin also needs **at least 20 Faith and trauma below 60**.

1. Arrange two lessons through the candidates' book: receiving a parishioner and inspecting wards, **thirty minutes each**.
2. After accepting the warehouse or shore assignment, invite companions to meet on site before setting out. Leave **ninety minutes** free.
3. Helping with a rescue, evidence or dangerous ritual earns appointment experience. Finish the assignment and report truthfully. After the shore investigation, you can also return with companions still needing experience to escort a witness or check records.
4. Accompany a **fifteen-minute interview**, then allow **six to seven hours** for the ordination trial. You can pause and continue later. With Robin, **09:00 on a school-free weekday** is a suitable start.

After appointment, Robin keeps the temple bunk and Sydney gains prayer-room access.

### Records room

Joining the rota opens building plans, delivery records and fieldwork records. First readings train History.

| Work                         | Cost                                 | Use                                                             |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Read records                 | Thirty minutes                       | Find clues                                                      |
| Repair the roof and drainage | £4,000, two days                     | Improves reading conditions together with new shelves           |
| Replace damp shelves         | £1,500, one day                      | Improves reading conditions together with roof repairs          |
| Dry and sort folios          | Thirty minutes daily, three sessions | History, Housekeeping and grace, with faster reading afterwards |

Both projects reduce reading to **twenty minutes**. Sorting the folios reduces it to **fifteen** and lets monks/nuns and priests copy ward patterns. Patterns reduce comparing marks to **ten minutes** and remove one tentacle. Fieldwork records shorten withdrawal to **three minutes**.

Borrow a lamp, rope and gloves from Jordan before setting out.

### Daily work

One paid shift daily, starting **by 17:00**, outside mass. Start reception **before 17:00**. Return inspections use the same daily shift.

| Work                                         | Time               | Main gains                                                           |
| -------------------------------------------- | ------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Count bowls and check receipts               | Forty-five minutes | £25, Maths, 1 grace and good fame                                    |
| Air blankets and record the handover         | Sixty minutes      | £30, Housekeeping, 2 grace and good fame                             |
| Supervise the next shift (priest)            | Forty-five minutes | £40, 2 grace and good fame                                           |
| Inspect the garden and repair wards (priest) | Sixty minutes      | £40, Tending, Housekeeping, 2 grace, garden condition +3–9           |
| Handover with Robin (both priests)           | Thirty minutes     | £30, Housekeeping, 1 grace, Robin's Faith and love, stress relief    |
| Check receipts with Sydney (both priests)    | Thirty minutes     | £35, History, Maths, 1 grace, Sydney's love, stress relief           |
| Receive people seeking help (priest)         | Thirty minutes     | £40, 2 grace, English or History practice depending on your approach |

Reception brings different visitors each day. Willpower, English and History affect the strain of helping them. Afterwards, discuss the visit with Robin or Sydney at the pews to hear their views.

When tired or hurt, rest in the warm room by the desk for **thirty minutes** once daily to ease pain, tiredness and stress.

### Private requests

- After contacting the bishop, accept the sealed note at the desk. Read the delivery records, finish the crate assignment and report truthfully. Copy a separate comparison, then deliver it to the bishop waiting as a confessor.
- As a priest who has heard the manor's history, accept the monstrance request. Read the index, then visit an available Kylar **18:00–23:00**, with **jealousy below 30** and no stalking or control. Record what Kylar agrees to share, then deliver it for **£60 and 2 History**, or keep it private. Completing Lost Lamb brings dream memories into the conversation.
