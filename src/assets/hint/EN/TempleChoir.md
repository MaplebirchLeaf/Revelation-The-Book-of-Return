### Prerequisites and recommended order

The choir is temple work organised by Jordan and adds the **Singing** skill. **Robin does not need to join the temple, and Robin's promise route is not required.**

- Enable **Temple choir**.
- Pass the original entrance assessment and become an **initiate, monk/nun or priest**. Applicants cannot join yet.
- Remain covered, have met Jordan, and reach the normal activity choices in the temple hall. Resolve any examination or story that currently takes priority.

Recommended order: **ask in the hall → practise daily → sing on Sundays → collect earnings at a monthly examination → improve base singing and qualify to lead**.

### Joining and daily practice

Choose **Ask about the choir (0:10)** in **Temple → Hall** and complete Jordan's audition to join. There is no minimum singing grade. Beginners are accepted too. Better singing changes Jordan's response. Joining gives singing practice and grace.

Return to the **temple hall** for **Attend choir practice**. Practice is available once daily from **09:00 to 18:00**, except during Sunday mass, **11:00–13:00**. Open the practice page and choose one exercise. The exercise takes thirty minutes.

| Exercise                | Effects                                                           | When to choose it                                   |
| ----------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| Practise breathing      | Improves singing, adds slight tiredness and eases stress          | When you also need to settle your nerves            |
| Match the pitch         | The largest singing improvement of the three, with more tiredness | When rested and prioritising base skill             |
| Rehearse with the choir | Improves singing, adds slight tiredness and gives grace           | When building temple contribution alongside singing |

All three share the daily practice allowance. Merely opening the practice page does not count. Choosing an exercise records it. **Practice within the last two days** helps your Sunday performance. Older practice no longer provides this benefit.

### Practice outside the temple

With the choir module enabled, these activities train singing even before you join the temple or choir.

- **Pub musician:** The native full-harpy **Sing along** option adds 10 base singing points. Tipping and listening do not train singing; the musician retains the native weekly availability rules.
- **Tower perch:** The native **Sing (0:30)** action adds 8 base singing points. The Great Hawk’s response still follows the native schedule, transformation and relationship conditions. Singing alone also provides practice.
- **Harpy bonus:** At transformation level 3, current singing performance receives a 20% bonus. This does not add base skill or replace the requirements for leading the choir.

Practice respects the skill cap and stat freeze. These activities do not count as choir rehearsals or earn temple pay.

### Singing grades and performance

Find **Singing** under **Character → Characteristics → Skills**. It uses the original skill-card grades: **None, F, F+, D, D+, C, C+, B, B+, A, A+, S**, with S as the highest grade.

Practice, the audition and actual singing during services improve the base skill. With Robin's temple route enabled, a fully transformed harpy can also sing for Robin at the temple bench for additional practice.

| Current condition                                                | Effect on singing                                          |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| Harpy transformation developed enough to provide its skill bonus | Improves performance without requiring full transformation |
| Significant tiredness                                            | Reduces performance, so rest beforehand                    |
| Alcohol                                                          | Reduces performance and can combine with tiredness         |
| Practice within the last two days                                | Helps keep each verse steady                               |

The skill card lists harpy, tiredness and alcohol modifiers. **Base skill** and **current performance** are different. Leading requires a base grade of B. A harpy bonus does not replace training the base skill.

### Sunday service: entrance and steps

On **Sunday, 11:00–13:00**, choose **Sing with the choir (1:00)** in the hall. You can participate once that day. Having attended ordinary mass already prevents another choir service.

1. Sing the **opening hymn, response to the reading and closing hymn**, twenty minutes each, for one hour in total.
2. Choose **Follow the choir** or **Support the harmony** each round. **Lead the verse** becomes available after approval. You can change style between rounds.
3. Read the feedback and current performance score. Missing a line does not end the service. The choir covers your part.
4. Finish the third round and hear Jordan's assessment so the work is recorded, then return to the hall.

| Style               | Advice                                               | Points for a clear verse |
| ------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Follow the choir    | Best for beginners, with the lowest requirement      | 2                        |
| Support the harmony | More demanding, better once the melody is familiar   | 2                        |
| Lead the verse      | Requires Jordan's approval and is the most demanding | 3                        |

An unsteady verse earns one point in any style. A lost verse earns none. Skill, recent practice and your condition affect the result. A higher grade does not guarantee every verse. Each round adds tiredness. Keeping up, even unsteadily, eases a little stress. Losing your part adds stress.

Finishing the service also gives **purity and grace**, with good performance increasing Jordan's love. Even a poor performance records the work and minimum bonus if you complete all three rounds.

<span class="red">Starting choir service uses today's mass opportunity. Leaving early gives no bonus for this service, and you cannot restart that day.</span> Previously recorded earnings remain.

### Qualifying to lead

Jordan approves leading **at the end of a completed service** when all these conditions are met:

- At least **three completed daily practices**.
- At least **three completed services**. Leaving early does not count.
- **Base singing at grade B or above**.
- At least **five performance points in this service**.

The qualifying third service counts towards attendance. Leading becomes available at the **next service**, without another application. Reaching B alone is insufficient without attendance and performance.

### Earnings and collection

A completed service adds **£70** to your monthly allowance, plus **£20 per performance point**.

| Performance                                    | Recorded earnings |
| ---------------------------------------------- | ----------------- |
| 0 points, but completed                        | £70               |
| 5 points                                       | £170              |
| 6 points, the maximum for following or harmony | £190              |
| 9 points, three clear lead verses              | £250              |

Mixed styles use the actual total score. These are **uncollected choir earnings**, not an immediate payment into your wallet each Sunday.

Attend the monthly temple examination when due. **Passing and receiving the allowance** pays the choir earnings alongside it. The original allowance still depends on grace. A failed examination or an allowance withheld for insufficient grace leaves the choir earnings pending until a later payment. Examinations with Robin or both promise partners use the same balance.

Check **Journal → Temple choir** for uncollected earnings, practice and service counts, permission to lead and the schedule.

### Common problems

| Problem                             | What to check                                                                                             |
| ----------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| No option to ask about the choir    | Module enabled, eligible temple rank, covered clothing, and no original event taking priority in the hall |
| No practice option                  | Joined already, 09:00–18:00, no practice completed today, outside Sunday mass                             |
| No Sunday service option            | 11:00–13:00, no ordinary mass or choir service attended today, still eligible and covered                 |
| High displayed skill but no leading | Base grade B or above, three practices, three completed services and five points in the current service   |
| No money after singing              | Check the journal balance and collect it after passing a monthly examination with an allowance payment    |
| Missed a verse                      | Continue the remaining rounds, since completing a low-scoring service still earns the minimum bonus       |
| No singing growth                   | Check whether the game's stat freeze is enabled                                                           |
