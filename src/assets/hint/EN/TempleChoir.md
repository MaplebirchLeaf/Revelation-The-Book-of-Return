# Temple choir · Singing

Join the choir, practise daily and sing at Sunday mass. With enough experience, Jordan will let you lead. You can also perform at the park and pub for tips.

### Joining and practice

As an initiate, monk/nun or priest, visit the **temple hall** in suitable clothing and **Ask about the choir (0:10)**. Beginners can join.

Choose one **thirty-minute** exercise daily, **09:00–18:00**, outside Sunday mass:

| Exercise                | Effect                                         |
| ----------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| Practise breathing      | 12 Singing, slight tiredness and stress relief |
| Match the pitch         | 18 Singing and slight tiredness                |
| Rehearse with the choir | 15 Singing, slight tiredness and grace         |

Practice within the last **two days** makes Sunday's singing easier.

### Other practice and performances

Joining or leading a pirate shanty, singing with the pub musician as a full harpy, and singing on the tower perch all train Singing. The practice count for choir leadership comes from daily temple exercises.

Find Singing under **Character → Characteristics → Skills**. Harpy transformation level **3** gives **20%** better effective Singing. Tiredness at half maximum and drunkenness each reduce performance by **10%**.

- **Park:** perform for passers-by during daytime without rain.
- **Smugglers' pub:** offer to sing while the musician is present.
- The two venues share **one performance daily**. A set has three **ten-minute** segments. Keep the melody steady, add a flourish or invite the listeners to sing. Singing, English and audience interest affect the result.
- A full set earns about **£15–60 at the pub or £6–36 in the park**. Finish early to collect tips for the completed portion. Leaving directly or being interrupted loses uncollected tips. Good performances also improve good fame.
- At Deadwood Reblooms property banquets, you can sing for the guests, affecting their interest and relationships.

### Sunday mass

On **Sunday, 11:00–13:00**, choose **Sing with the choir (1:00)** in the hall. Sing three verses, **twenty minutes each**.

Following the melody is easier. Harmony needs more skill, and approved singers can lead. A clear verse earns **2 points**, or **3 when leading**. An unsteady verse earns **1 point**.

Joining brings the mass's holy effects: full angels gain stress and trauma relief, while fallen angels and full demons gain stress, trauma and pain. Fallen angels also gain more purity. These effects remain if you leave early.

You can attend mass once daily. Finish the service and hear Jordan's assessment to record attendance, grace and earnings. Good performance also improves Jordan's affection.

### Leading and payment

At the end of a completed service, qualify to lead by meeting all of these:

- Complete **3 daily practices**.
- Complete **3 choir services**, including the current one.
- Reach **base Singing B (600)**.
- Earn **at least 5 points** in the current service.

You can lead from the next Sunday. Each completed service records **£70 + £20 per point**, up to **£250**. Earnings accumulate and are paid with the temple allowance after a successful monthly examination. Any unpaid earnings remain pending.

The Journal records the schedule, leadership approval and pending earnings. Statistics shows completed practices and services.
