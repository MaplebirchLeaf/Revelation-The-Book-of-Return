### Joining and daily practice

Enable **Temple choir**, pass the temple entrance assessment and become an **initiate, monk/nun or priest**. Remain covered and meet Jordan. In **Temple → Hall**, choose **Ask about the choir (0:10)** when ordinary activities are available. Beginners can join; Robin's temple route is not required.

Recommended route: **join → practise daily → sing at Sunday services → collect earnings at monthly examinations → qualify to lead**.

**Choose a choir exercise** opens once daily, **09:00–18:00**, outside Sunday mass **11:00–13:00**. Choose one thirty-minute exercise:

| Exercise                | Effect                                                |
| ----------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| Practise breathing      | Singing practice, slight tiredness and stress relief. |
| Match the pitch         | The greatest singing gain, with more tiredness.       |
| Rehearse with the choir | Singing practice, slight tiredness and grace.         |

All three share the daily allowance. Opening the page alone uses none of it. Practice within the last **two days** improves Sunday performance.

### Singing outside the temple

These activities train singing while the module is enabled, even before joining:

| Activity                                           | Base singing gain |
| -------------------------------------------------- | ----------------- |
| Pirate deck: join a shanty / attempt to lead       | 6 / 10            |
| Pub musician: the full-harpy **Sing along** option | 10                |
| Tower perch: **Sing (0:30)**                       | 8                 |

Musician availability and the Great Hawk's response retain their usual conditions. These actions do not count as choir rehearsals or earn temple pay. With Robin's temple route, a full harpy can also sing for Robin at the temple bench.

Leading a pirate shanty uses the higher of effective Singing and English for the existing check. Success keeps the native respect and trauma effects; the harpy's special song remains available.

### Paid sets and private performances

Offer to sing while the pub musician is available, or perform for passers-by in the park during daytime without rain. Ordinary PCs can participate. The two venues share one daily opportunity. A full set has **three ten-minute segments**.

- **Keep the melody steady**: easier to sustain; one point for a clear segment.
- **Add a flourish**: more demanding and tiring; three points when clear.
- **Invite the listeners into the chorus**: English helps; two points when clear.

Effective Singing, English, audience interest and a small variation affect each result. Unsteady or lost segments lower the score and audience interest. Three segments finish the set automatically; you may also finish early and collect tips for the completed portion.

A full set pays **£15–60 at the pub or £6–36 in the park**, directly to your wallet. Good performances also improve good fame. Leaving without finishing, or a forced interruption, forfeits uncollected tips.

With Deadwood's property module, singing can also entertain private banquet guests. It uses the same interaction and the banquet's existing approval, relationships and hospitality settlement. Hosting your own party does not pay tips.

Find **Singing** in **Character → Characteristics → Skills**. Training raises base skill; current performance also includes a **20% harpy bonus from transformation level 3**, a tiredness penalty from half maximum tiredness, and an alcohol penalty. Tiredness and alcohol penalties can combine. Stat freeze prevents skill growth.

### Sunday service

On **Sunday, 11:00–13:00**, choose **Sing with the choir (1:00)** in the hall. Sing the opening hymn, response and closing hymn, **twenty minutes each**. Choose **Follow the choir**, **Support the harmony**, or, after approval, **Lead the verse**. You can change style between rounds.

Following is easiest; harmony and leading demand more skill. A clear verse earns two points, or three when leading. An unsteady verse earns one; a lost verse earns none. Recent practice, effective singing and a small random variation affect the result. Each round adds tiredness. Keeping up eases some stress; losing your part adds stress.

Entry applies the original mass's purity and transformation effects once, checking angel first, then fallen angel, then demon. A full angel receives trauma and stress relief; fallen angels and full demons gain trauma, stress and pain. The fallen angel's greater purity gain is retained. Leaving early does not undo these effects.

Complete all three rounds and hear Jordan's assessment to record attendance and earnings. Finishing gives grace; good performance improves Jordan's affection.

**Starting uses today's mass opportunity.** Ordinary mass attended earlier prevents choir service. Leaving early gives no bonus and you cannot restart that day. Previously recorded earnings remain.

### Leading and the monthly allowance

At the end of a completed service, Jordan approves leading when you have:

- **Three completed daily practices**.
- **Three completed services**, including the current one.
- **Base Singing B or above**.
- **At least five performance points** in the current service.

Leading becomes available next Sunday. A transformation bonus does not replace the base-skill requirement.

A completed service records **£70 + £20 per performance point**: £70 at zero points, £170 at five, £190 at six, or £250 at nine. These earnings accumulate for your **monthly temple allowance**.

Pass the monthly examination and receive an allowance to collect them. A failed examination or allowance withheld for insufficient grace leaves them pending. Examinations with Robin, or both promise partners, use the same balance. Robin's weekly income and Finance's relief fund remain separate.

The Journal records the schedule, approval to lead and unpaid choir earnings. Completed practice and service counts are shown in Statistics.
