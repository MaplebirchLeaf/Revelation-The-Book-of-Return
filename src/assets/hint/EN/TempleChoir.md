### Joining and practising

After joining the temple, ask Jordan about the choir in the **main hall**. You must remain covered and Jordan must be available. No singing experience is required, and Robin's temple route is optional.

Attend one half-hour practice per day between **09:00 and 18:00**, except during Sunday mass from 11:00 to 13:00. Your journal records practice counts and the next service.

### Sunday service

Choose **Sing with the choir** in the hall on **Sundays from 11:00 to 13:00**. A service has three rounds and takes one hour. Following suits beginners, harmony demands more skill, and leading requires Jordan's approval.

- A completed service adds **£70** plus **£20 per performance point** to your monthly allowance. Following or supporting harmony can add up to **£190** per service, and leading up to **£250**.
- A clear verse earns two points when following or harmonising, or three when leading. An unsteady verse earns one point, and a missed verse earns none. Your current score appears beside the service choices.
- Reach 600 base singing, complete at least three practices and three services, and score at least five in the current service to gain approval to lead.
- Choir work counts as attending that day's mass. You cannot attend ordinary mass or sing another choir service that day.
- The final hymn records the bonus. **Collect it with the native temple allowance after a successful monthly examination.** The native amount still depends on grace. If your examination fails or your allowance is withheld, choir earnings remain pending until you qualify for payment. The journal shows uncollected earnings.
- Leaving early earns no bonus for that service and prevents restarting that day. Previous earnings remain intact.

### Singing skill

Singing starts at 0 and caps at 1,000. Practice and work improve it, and practice within the last two days also helps during service. Stage three harpy transformation gives a **20% bonus**. Fatigue and alcohol each reduce current performance. The skill page lists active bonuses and penalties.

<span class="teal">A mistake does not end your shift. Finish with the choir, then practise the melody before attempting harmony or leading.</span>
