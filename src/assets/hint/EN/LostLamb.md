### Prerequisites and unlock order

**Lost lamb** continues the original monstrance discovery at Kylar's manor with later conversations and a desk-tidying scene. Robin's temple route and choir membership are not required.

1. Enable **Lost lamb**.
2. Unlock manor visits through the original route, help clean the manor and earn the parents' full trust.
3. Talk to Kylar at the manor and use the original **Tell Kylar that the manor is clean** option. Follow the visit to the secret upstairs.
4. **Finish the discovery and return to Kylar's bedroom.** Starting the investigation or seeing the monstrance alone is insufficient. The module records the experience on the original final page.
5. Later, visit **Kylar's manor → Bedroom → Talk to Kylar**, between **18:00 and 23:00**, for **Ask about the monstrance**. Kylar must be able to act normally, and the PC must be outside combat.

<span class="teal">If Kylar is offering the original camera gift, open or refuse it before returning to ordinary conversation.</span> The new topic does not bypass that event.

| ----------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |

| Tell Kylar what the bishop said about the manor | Heard the full revelation in this save, the original **Pagan Rite** discovery | Relay the bishop's account of the parents and temple involvement       |
| Insist the temple should investigate            | Finished the manor discovery                                                  | Kylar rejects temple involvement and closes the topic                  |

Report the discovery through **Temple → Talk to Jordan → Enquire about the monstrance you found in Kylar's manor**. The bishop's full account requires progressing the original subsequent encounter. Reporting alone does not unlock it. Once you have heard that account, the bishop option replaces the Jordan-report option.

Your temple membership and Kylar's current jealousy change some responses. **The conversation records your stance. It currently gives no direct love, jealousy or faith changes, does not recruit Kylar to the temple and does not resolve the parents' fate.**

You can return and choose another stance. Insisting on an investigation does not permanently close the route, but it prevents desk tidying after that conversation.

### Tidying the desk: entrance and effects

After a conversation, return to ordinary conversation for **Stay and tidy the desk together (0:15)**, provided your latest stance is not insisting on temple investigation. This can be completed once per save.

Clear the space beside the keyboard and organise Kylar's papers together. Later conversation pages mention the space kept clear. This is a follow-up scene, without money or an additional feat. It does not investigate the computer or solve the family mystery.

### Connections to other routes

- **Confounded Vow** also reads the original Pagan Rite discovery in this save. It does not require this conversation or the desk scene. Hearing the bishop does not by itself meet the two partners' ritual conditions.
- Deadwood Reblooms' Kylar expansion is not required. Disappearance, imprisonment or inability to act caused by other modules still affects normal visits.
- The final original discovery page must be visited with this module enabled. Earlier completed experiences are not automatically backfilled, and progress is not inferred from feats earned in another save.
